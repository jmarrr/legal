Hourlamp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to Hourlamp, an idle fantasy game for iOS.

Short version: your game save stays on your phone. The only thing Hourlamp sends is a few anonymous daily counts, such as how many players came back today. There are no accounts and no device or advertising identifiers, and nothing sent can be traced to you or your phone. You can turn the counts off in More → Settings → Share play counts.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, settings and purchase status are stored in the app's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us and we have no way to retrieve them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous play counts

To learn whether players come back and where they stop, so we know what to fix, Hourlamp sends daily counts of play events to our own counter. Each count contains only:

  • the event and how many times it happened that day: days played, sessions started, game milestones (for example reaching a floor, a first Relight or a first purchase) and error counts;
  • the date it happened;
  • the date the app was first opened, which is shared by everyone who installed on that day;
  • the app version and your phone's language.

Nothing else is sent. There is no identifier of any kind: no account, no device or advertising identifier, no install identifier, and no name or email. Counts from everyone who first opened the game on the same date are added together. On a date when only one person installed, that date's counts describe one anonymous player, but nothing in them says who it is. Because of this, no one's counts can be looked up or deleted individually.

Like any web request, the request that delivers the counts also carries standard technical details, such as your IP address and a generic iOS network label. Our code stores none of them. The request may briefly appear in our hosting provider's request logs, and we do not keep those logs.

The counts are stored as totals in a database run by our hosting providers, Vercel and Upstash, and expire after 400 days. They are used only to improve the game. We never sell or share them, never use them for advertising, and never combine them with data from other apps or companies. Hourlamp does not track you as Apple defines tracking.

Turning the counts off

Go to More → Settings → Share play counts and switch it off. From that moment nothing is sent, and anything waiting to be sent is cleared from your phone.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, logins or profiles.
  • No advertising, and no advertising, attribution or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No third-party payment or subscription provider.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Purchases

Hourlamp offers optional one-time in-app purchases. They are handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells the app on your phone what you own; we never see your name, email address, card details or Apple Account information. The anonymous counts above can include that a first purchase happened on a given day, with nothing that identifies who made it. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow it, Hourlamp schedules a local notification on your phone for when your lamp runs out. There are no push notifications, no push tokens and no server involved. Change it in Hourlamp's settings or in iOS Settings.

Save export and import

Hourlamp can copy your save as text so you can keep a backup or move it to another phone. It is copied only when you tap export, and it goes wherever you paste it, under that app's or service's policy, not ours.

Children

Hourlamp is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and the anonymous counts carry nothing that identifies a player.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, a request to access, correct or delete data will generally be answered by explaining that we hold none: your save is on your phone, and the counts can't be linked to you. If you have rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or similar laws, you may still contact us and we will respond.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the "last updated" date above. If a change is significant, for example sending anything beyond anonymous counts, we will say so clearly in the game before it takes effect.
