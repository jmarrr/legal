Health information and predictions

Wren's cycle predictions are estimates calculated from the dates you enter, and are shown as a range with a stated confidence precisely because they are uncertain. Cycles affected by PCOS are often irregular by nature, which makes prediction less reliable, not more. Reference ranges shown next to lab markers are typical adult ranges for context only — real ranges vary by laboratory, assay, age and cycle timing.
