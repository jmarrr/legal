Skintel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy applies to Skintel, a skin tracking app for iOS.

Short version: your photo is used to produce your score and is not kept afterwards. Your scores, routine and history stay on your device. We do not sell your data, we do not use it to train AI models, and there are no advertising SDKs in the app. Unlike a purely offline app, Skintel does have to process your photo to analyse it — this policy explains exactly how.

What Skintel stores on your device

The following is written to your device's local app storage and is not uploaded to us:

  • Your scores and readings — the overall score and the individual metrics (hydration, even tone, pores, fine lines) for each scan, with the date.
  • Your routine — which steps you have, and which you ticked off on which day.
  • Your skin profile — concerns, skin type, and any ingredient sensitivities you tell us about.
  • App settings — reminder preferences and whether onboarding is complete.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase it from within the app at any time.

Your photo, and what happens to it

This is the part that matters most, so it is spelled out in full.

  • When you take a scan, the photo is used solely to generate your skin analysis.
  • Where analysis runs on your device, the photo never leaves it.
  • Where analysis requires our AI processing provider, the image is transmitted over an encrypted connection, analysed, and the result returned. Under our agreement with that provider the image is not retained after processing and is not used to train any model.
  • We do not attach your name, email or any account identifier to the image, because Skintel has no accounts.
  • We do not keep a copy of your photo on any server of ours.

If you would rather not have a photo analysed at all, do not run a scan — the routine checklist and the rest of the app work without one.

Face data

An image of your face is personal information, and in some places it is treated as sensitive. Skintel does not perform facial recognition, does not attempt to identify you, does not match your face against any database, and does not build a face template or faceprint. The analysis measures visual characteristics of skin — texture, tone, evenness — and produces a score. Nothing about it is used to recognise a person.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no password.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No advertising SDKs and no cross-app tracking.
  • No use of your photos or readings to train AI models.
  • No health data written to Apple Health unless you explicitly ask for it.

Subscriptions

Skintel Premium is sold through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details. To manage entitlements we use RevenueCat, which receives a random, app-generated identifier and your purchase status. It does not receive your photos, scores or any health information.

Analytics and crash reporting

If crash or usage reporting is enabled in a future version, it will record only technical events — that a screen opened, that a purchase completed, that an error occurred. Your photos, scores and readings are never included in analytics.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a push server and cannot send you anything.

Your controls

  • Delete everything — Settings has a control that erases your scans, routine history and profile from the device immediately.
  • Camera permission — you can revoke it in iOS Settings at any time; the rest of the app continues to work.
  • Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

Because we hold no account and no server-side copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request — but if you have a question about it, write to us and we will answer.

Children

Skintel is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app to take a scan, contact us and we will help.

Skintel is not medical advice

Skintel is a cosmetic tracking tool. It does not diagnose skin conditions, does not detect skin cancer or any other disease, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. A score is an estimate from a photograph. Speak to a clinician about anything that concerns you.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle your photo or your data changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated. Material changes will also be surfaced in the app.
