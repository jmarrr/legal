What Skintel does

Skintel analyses a photograph you take and produces a score with a breakdown across several visual characteristics of skin. It also keeps a routine checklist and a history of your scans so you can see change over time.

Scores are estimates

A score is an estimate produced by software from a single photograph. It is affected by lighting, camera quality, angle, makeup and expression. Two scans taken minutes apart can differ. Scores are for your own tracking and interest — they are not measurements of health, and they are not a clinical assessment. Where Skintel is not confident in a reading, it says so; treat a score accordingly.

Not medical advice

Nothing in Skintel — the score, the breakdown, the routine, or any educational text — is medical advice. Skintel does not diagnose acne, rosacea, eczema, melanoma or any other condition, and it cannot tell you whether a mark on your skin is dangerous. If you have a skin condition, are pregnant, take prescription treatments, or notice a change that concerns you, speak to a dermatologist or doctor. Never delay seeking medical advice because of something this app showed you.
