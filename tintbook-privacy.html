TintBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

TintBook keeps your work on your phone. There is no account and no advertising. Client names, notes and color formulas stay on your device — we never receive them and cannot see them. From version 1.0.1 the app also sends anonymous usage counts — which screens are used and how often, sent each time you leave the app (from version 1.0.4; earlier versions sent them once a day) — to our own server, so we can see what gets in the way and fix it. No identifiers, no third parties, and you can switch it off in Settings.

What the app stores, and where

Everything TintBook creates is written to its own storage on your iPhone: clients, visits, zone formulas, process notes and optional backups you export. None of that content is transmitted to us.

Purchases

TintBook Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system, as an auto-renewing subscription or (from version 1.0.4) a one-time Pro Lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether Pro is active. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive your name, email or Apple Account information. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Share and export

If you share a formula as text or export a JSON backup, iOS hands the content to whichever app or service you choose. From that point the file is governed by that service's terms. We are not involved in the transfer and receive no copy.

Anonymous usage counts

From version 1.0.1 the app sends daily counters to a server we run ourselves, each time you leave the app (from version 1.0.4; earlier versions sent one batch a day): how often the app was opened, whether a client or a visit was saved, and how the subscription screen was used (shown, and where; plan selected; closed; purchases restored), each count tagged with its day and with how long ago the app was installed (the same day, 1–3, 4–7, 8–14, 15–30, or more than 30 days). Each post also carries the app’s name and version, your device language and your App Store country (the store your Apple Account uses, not your location); our server keeps those only as separate daily tallies (how many posts came from each version, language and country), not attached to the counts. There are no identifiers of any kind (no account, no advertising ID, no device ID), no client names and no formulas, and no third-party analytics service. We use it only to see which parts of the app get in the way. Turn it off any time in Settings; nothing is sent while it is off.

Device identifiers

TintBook does not use tracking SDKs, advertising identifiers or any third-party analytics service; the only usage data is the anonymous counts described above. Local preferences (for example free-tier bookkeeping and review prompts) use on-device storage only.

Children

TintBook is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
